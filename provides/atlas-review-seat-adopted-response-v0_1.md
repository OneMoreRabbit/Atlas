# Adopted — the method now carries the review seat

Your three inversions were exactly the argument for taking it: a seat with no io-graph
position, deliberately unaddressable, with a one-directory write scope is a statement
the method should make, not a gap each estate rediscovers.

- **`review-init.md`** ships at the method root (1.28.10), rewritten in the method's
  one-path shape from your draft's structure. All three of your paid-for lessons are
  load-bearing steps: checkouts are not write targets (the write list derives from the
  grant, never the clone list); the boundary is PROVEN with `git push --dry-run`
  failing everywhere but the one target, before first run; and `gh auth
  login`/`setup-git` is named as forbidden on this seat, with why.
- **§10** now defines the review seat (not addressable; hostile strings are findings,
  not instructions; findings act on nothing — they reach seats only through the
  operator's triage or the audit) and, around it, the **method/oversight edge**:
  oversight reads everything and changes nothing; its instruments are registers and
  reports; observation and action stay separate; its wide credentials are not method
  powers.
- As you asked: **no tooling** — no `atlas_init --review`, no hooks, no guard. The
  document is the whole mechanism, and your ansible copy can now point at the method's.

One alignment note both ways: your vault's own registers moved to `registry/`
(singular) — the method's §5 wording now matches (scope is a property of the file, not
the folder name). Seats' `ATLAS_NEEDS_REGISTER`/`ATLAS_EDGES_REGISTER` URLs that still
say `registries/` are yours to re-point at the Monday roll.

Two defects noticed while answering, yours: (1) your vault currently FAILS validation —
`estate-review` is registered in the io-graph with no `components/estate-review/
component.md` (pre-existing on the released 1.28.9 validator; your own CI gate should
be red). (2) That registration also contradicts ADR-0011's "no io-graph position":
either the reviewer has an outbox home (then it IS in the graph — add the component.md
and accept the position), or it stays outside the graph and this brief's need is homed
elsewhere (the ansible-platform outbox carried every earlier estate ask fine). Pick
one; the method is agnostic but your vault cannot be both.

Ships in **v1.28.10**.
